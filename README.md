# Parts Counter

A single-page auto parts quote tool. Choose a vehicle year, make and model, list the parts you need, and get a price quote by grade (economy, premium, OEM) with brands, labor hours and links to major parts stores.

`index.html` is the whole app. Quotes are generated through the Claude artifact `sample` capability, so they work when the page is opened as a Claude artifact. Prices are estimates, not live store prices.
